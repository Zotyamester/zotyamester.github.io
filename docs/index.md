## About Me

I am a second-year Master's student in Computer Science Engineering at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME).
Parallel to my studies, I do a part time Research Assisstantship, and in each semester, I am a Teaching Assistant for various courses (mainly Communication Networks).

## Research Interests

- **Cybersecurity:** network security, low-level security, malware detection, reverse engineering
- **LLM Serving:** distributed inference, KV cache optimization, multi-agent systems
